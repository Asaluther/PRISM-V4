The history of artificial intelligence began in antiquity with myths and stories of artificial beings. The seeds of modern AI were planted by philosophers who described human thinking as mechanical symbol manipulation. This work culminated in the programmable digital computer in the 1940s. Those who attended the Dartmouth workshop in 1955 became leaders of AI research for decades. Many predicted a machine as intelligent as a human would exist within a generation. Researchers had grossly underestimated the difficulty of the project by 1974. The field went through cycles of optimism followed by disappointment and loss of funding. Very large scale integration led to increasingly powerful computers in the 1980s. Investment and interest in AI boomed in the first decades of the twenty first century. Machine learning was successfully applied to many problems in academia and industry. The success was due to large amounts of data and increased computer power. Deep learning became the dominant approach after breakthrough results in recognition. New architectures and training methods continue to be developed and refined regularly. The transformers architecture revolutionized natural language processing starting in 2017. Large language models like GPT showed remarkable capabilities in text generation. These models are trained on massive datasets and use attention mechanisms effectively. The field of artificial intelligence continues to advance at an unprecedented pace.
